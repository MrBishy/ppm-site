Privacy Policy

Play Pocket Manager for Android · Buckminster Interactive
Last updated 19 August 2026

Play Pocket Manager is a single-player football card game. This policy explains, plainly, what the Android app stores, what it sends, and what you can do about it.

The short version. The app shows no adverts, contains no analytics or tracking software, and asks for no account, name, email address or permissions. Your game is saved on your device. It is also backed up automatically to an anonymous cloud save so you do not lose your club. There is one optional purchase, handled entirely by Google Play — we never see your card details.

1. What is stored on your device

Your progress is kept in the app's own local storage. It never leaves your device except through the cloud save described in section 2.

WhatWhy
Your club, squad, card collection, coins, season and career historyso the game can continue where you left off
Your settings — sound, music, theme, layout, bigger text, reduce motionso your choices persist
A local play log of in-game eventsused only for diagnosing problems on your own device. It is never transmitted.
A copy of any save file the game could not readkept so a damaged save can be recovered rather than silently overwritten
Whether you have bought the full career unlockstored in encrypted app storage so you can play offline. It is a yes/no flag and nothing more.

Uninstalling the app removes all of it. Starting a new game from the in-game settings clears your progress on the device.

2. Cloud save

So that you do not lose your club, the app backs your save up to a hosted database provided by Supabase. This happens automatically when the app starts and after your game saves.

  • The app signs in anonymously. No email address, password, name or Google account details are used or requested.
  • Anonymous sign-in creates a random identifier for your installation. That identifier is the only thing linking a save to a device.
  • What is stored is your game save — club name, badge, squad, collection, coins, season, career record and settings. Nothing else.
  • Your club name is text you type yourself. Please do not put your real name, school, address or anything else personal into it.

Please read this if you are a parent. The cloud backup is on by default and is not something the player switches on. It stores game progress under a random identifier, not a person. If you would rather nothing at all left the device, the app can be used with the device offline — the game is fully playable without a network connection.

3. The World Board (optional leaderboard)

The app includes an optional public leaderboard. It is off unless you deliberately turn it on and agree to publish.

If you turn it on, these are published: your club name, club badge and colour, squad rating, matches played, wins, draws, seasons completed, titles, cups, a shareable squad code, and the anonymous identifier described above. Club names are filtered before publication.

Turning sharing off deletes your row — it does not merely stop updating it.

4. Sending feedback

The app has a feedback box. It sends something only when you write a message and press Send.

These fields are sent, and no others: the feedback type you choose, your message, the app build and version number, which screen you were on, your browser engine and device type as reported by the system, and the window size. Your save, save code, squad, collection, club name and contact details are never included. Because no contact detail is sent, we cannot reply to feedback — please use the email address in section 8 if you need an answer.

5. Purchases

The app is free to download and the whole of your first season is free. Continuing into Season 2 requires a single permanent purchase.

  • The purchase is handled entirely by Google Play. Payment is processed by Google, under Google's privacy policy.
  • The app never sees or stores your payment details — no card number, no billing address, no name.
  • All the app receives from Google is whether the unlock has been purchased.
  • It is a one-off purchase. It is not a subscription, it never renews, and it cannot charge you automatically.
  • The purchase unlocks the rest of the career. It buys no cards, coins or advantages.

6. What the app does not do

  • No adverts, and no advertising networks.
  • No analytics, telemetry, crash-reporting or tracking software of any kind.
  • No advertising ID, device ID, IMEI, phone number or MAC address is collected.
  • No location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, files or calendar access. The app requests no runtime permissions.
  • No account, sign-up, email address or password.
  • Nothing is sold, rented or shared with advertisers or data brokers.

The app declares one permission, com.android.vending.BILLING, which exists solely so Google Play can process the optional purchase.

7. Children

The game is suitable for children and contains no adverts and no manipulative monetisation. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. The purchase screen asks the player to check with a grown-up before buying. Parents can control or disable purchases using Google Play's parental controls.

8. Deleting your data, and contacting us

What you can delete yourself

  • Everything on the device — uninstall the app, or start a new game from the in-game settings.
  • Your leaderboard entry — turn World Board sharing off in the game. The row is deleted.

What to ask us to delete

There is currently no in-app button that deletes your cloud save or a feedback message from our records. To have either removed, email the address below. Because sign-in is anonymous, please include your club name and, if you can, your squad code from the World Board panel, so the right record can be found. We will confirm once it is done.

9. Where data is held

Cloud saves, leaderboard entries and feedback are held by Supabase, our hosting provider, and are protected by row-level access rules so that one player cannot read another's save. Purchases are handled by Google. We do not transfer data to anyone else.

10. Changes to this policy

If what the app collects changes, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change. Material changes will be noted in the app's release notes.
