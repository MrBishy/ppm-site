Play Pocket Manager

A six-a-side football card game for Android.

Collect footballers, build a squad, manage chemistry and formation, play matches and climb the pyramid. There are no adverts and nothing to buy inside the game — no card packs, no coins, no advantages. Your first season is free, and one optional permanent unlock continues the career.
