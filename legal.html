Real footballers

The player cards in Play Pocket Manager use the names of real footballers. Names are used only to identify the player each card represents in an entertainment game. We do not claim ownership of any third-party name or trade mark, and all such rights stay with their owners.

A player's name appearing on a card does not mean that player, or anyone who represents them, has taken part in, approved or endorsed the game.

Clubs and competitions

Every club in the game is invented, with its own name, colours and badge. The game's divisions and the Pocket Cup are its own competitions. Play Pocket Manager does not use the names, crests or branding of real clubs, leagues or competitions.

No photographs or likenesses

The game does not include player photographs or likenesses, official club crests, competition or federation logos, or official kit designs. Cards show a simple illustrated shirt, not a real kit.

Simulation data

Some details on a card are ordinary football facts, such as a player's name, nationality and playing position.

Everything else is made for the game. Ratings, attributes, card tiers, traits, chemistry, form, injuries, match events and results are part of the simulation. They are not official assessments by any player, club or governing body, and they are not predictions about any real person. Something that happens in your saved game has not happened, will not necessarily happen, and is not alleged to have happened in real life.

Where the information comes from

Factual details such as names, nationalities and positions come from publicly available sources. Ratings and other game values are our own and are not taken from any other game or commercial database.

We try to get the facts right but cannot promise every detail is complete or current. If you spot a mistake, email buckminsterinteractive@gmail.com and we will look at correcting it in a future update.
